## Delegation and budget
Structure and validation run as deterministic local scripts. A lower-cost available model may author structural changes; a stronger available model may propose polish after the structural lock. Concurrency separates worker directories, output files and named responsibilities. A single integrator admits assets after manifest/geometry checks. Never share writable .blend files, expose an arbitrary-code MCP socket publicly, promise unlimited model quota or claim savings without measured before/after usage.
